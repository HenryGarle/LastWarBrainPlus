## Activity numbers (what "active" means) - 2026-09-28
- Active = logged in (last_seen not inside a mass touch AND not the first value we ever saw for that account - first_look, 2026-09-30), donated, or chatted. player_activity.last_active_at = GREATEST of the three. Power rises are NOT activity (96% of members rise passively).

## War game by hour / activity score - 2026-09-28
- Matchup (Compare > Alliances, two picked) has byHour: each member weighted by OnByHour = share of observed days (since first profile reading, max 14) with a moment in that UTC hour (neighbouring hour = half). Same attack/open-fight rules as the recency Matchup.

- Overview topAlliances: top 5 alliances per server by newest-profile power of members from that server; page shows rest as "other".
- Fighting strength (hero est x recency 1/0.75/0.35/0.1) is a Rankings metric for players/alliances/servers (recency from the profile's own last_seen at that snapshot, touches = 0.1) and a front-page measure (recency from player_activity now).
- War game table shows per hour, per side: rally (top 10 on), garrison (top 20 on), attacks %, defends % (= 1 - other side's attack).
- Research 2026-09-28 (lastwarhandbook, lastwarvault, theriagames): wall holds up to 5 squads incl. reinforcements; damage linear in troops; type counter +-20% (~40-44% swing); morale +1% dmg per 1% morale lead (cap +200%); handbook rule of thumb: 25% more power for a reliable win; online targets can shield/relocate/reinforce - attack when they are offline. No source for our flat +10% home bonus. Offline bases still defend with their own wall squads.
- Alliance war = Siege.cs (Matchup "siege", ?slots=100|20): one building the whole defending side garrisons, 1 squad per defender, march stops after 5 kills, timed waves (1st/2nd/3rd squads), online defenders re-garrison between waves, square-law duels, per-duel luck 0.2 + side luck 0.25. Scheduled war = recency weights; by hour = recency x own daily shape.
